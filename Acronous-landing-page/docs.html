Acronous Documentation

Everything you need to use Acronous AI, Navigwiz, Equyvo, the Acronous One bundle and the developer API. One account works across the whole ecosystem.

Getting started

1. Create one Acronous account at /signup (or in any app). The same login works on ai.acronous.com, navigwiz.acronous.com and equyvo.acronous.com.

2. Pick your starting point:

  • Chat & create → Acronous AI (free tier included, no card required).
  • Browse with AI → Navigwiz (the browser itself is free forever).
  • Post & grow → Equyvo (social is free; creators can upgrade).

3. Upgrade only when you need more. Paid plans unlock higher limits, premium models, agents and API credits. See pricing.

Accounts & sign-in

  • Sign-in is centralized: one JWT session cookie (acronous_token) valid across *.acronous.com for 7 days.
  • Each product app also accepts ?token=… once after login, then stores it locally.
  • Forgot access? Use /login → your email + password. There is no passwordless magic link today.
  • Signing out in any app calls POST /api/auth/logout and clears the shared cookie.

API overview

Use the Acronous AI brain inside your own apps via https://api.acronous.com — chat, vision, search and generation, metered per request. Full playground, keys and code samples live on the API page.

curl https://api.acronous.com/v1/chat \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-Api-Key: ak_YOUR_KEY" \
  -d '{"message":"Summarize this quarter in 3 bullets","session_id":"demo"}'

API keys

  • Create keys while signed in: POST /v1/api/keys {"name":"my-app"} with your JWT. The secret is shown once — store it server-side.
  • Send it as X-Api-Key (recommended) or Authorization: Bearer ak_….
  • List (GET /v1/api/keys) shows prefixes only; revoke with DELETE /v1/api/keys {"key_id":"ak_…"}.
  • 🔑 Keys are bearer credentials. Never ship them in mobile apps, client-side JS, or public repos — call api.acronous.com from your backend.

Subscriptions & billing

  • All checkout goes through Razorpay: UPI, credit/debit cards, netbanking, wallets, EMI and international cards.
  • Plans are monthly (30 days). Renew by purchasing again before expiry; status shows in each app and at GET /v1/billing/status.
  • You are never shown our bank details or QR codes — every payment is a Razorpay order created server-side and verified by HMAC signature. That is what makes it safe.
  • Enterprise / Business tiers are custom — enterprise@acronous.com.

Refunds & cancellation

  • Cancel anytime — your plan stays active until the end of the 30-day window, then drops to free. No lock-in.
  • Duplicate charges and failed-but-debited payments are refunded in full after verification (5–7 business days via Razorpay to source).
  • Digital-subscription payments already consumed (e.g. a month of Ultra heavily used) are non-refundable; unused accidental purchases within 48h are reviewed case-by-case at billing@acronous.com.

Security & privacy

  • Card/UPI data is entered only on Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant checkout — it never touches Acronous servers.
  • RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET lives in server secrets (Cloudflare) only; the browser only ever sees the public key id + order id.
  • Payment verification is HMAC-SHA256 over order_id|payment_id; forged callbacks fail closed.
  • Auth sessions are 7-day JWTs scoped to *.acronous.com; API keys are hashed at rest and shown once.

Fair use

“Very high allowance” means exactly that — Ultra and AI Ultra are large but finite, because inference has real cost. Automated abuse, resale of free-tier access, or scraping that degrades the service for others may be rate-limited or suspended. Enterprise tiers exist for bulk needs.

FAQ

Why not one identical plan for all three apps?

Because the value is different: Acronous AI sells intelligence/compute, Navigwiz sells AI work on the web, Equyvo sells premium social + creator earnings. Identical pricing would overcharge one audience and starve another.

Which plan should most people buy?

Acronous AI Plus (₹449) and Navigwiz AI Plus (₹299) are the mainstream plans the business is built around. Ultra tiers exist for heavy consumers, not as the default.

Is Ultra really unlimited?

No — and that is deliberate. Even the largest AI products meter heavy use. Ultra is a very large allowance with priority infrastructure.

How do I pay from outside India?

Razorpay accepts international cards on the same checkout. Pricing is in INR; your bank converts.

Do subscriptions auto-renew?

Today plans are manual-renew (buy again each 30 days) while UPI Autopay is being rolled out. Your data and history are kept either way.
